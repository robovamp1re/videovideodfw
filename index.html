FAQs

We bet you wonder why we're doing this.

Who are you?

We're a married couple that loves movies.

 

Why a Video Store?

We're lifelong movie fans and aficionados that miss the physical feel in an all digital world. Also, because we can!

 

What will your store offer?

We plan to offer DVDs, Blu-Rays, 4K UHD discs, and VHS tapes to rent or purchase, alongside some unique and interesting movie related merch. We're exploring options for rental club memberships, including access to communtiy events and screenings, along with rentals of individual titles for non-members.

Also, snacks :)

 

Where will it be?

We're working on figuring that out. If you have any suggestions, please drop us a line.

 

When will it open?

We're currently aiming for summer/fall of 2027.

 

How can I find out more?

Sign up for our membership waitlist!
